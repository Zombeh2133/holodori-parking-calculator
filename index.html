Calculation order

The math used by this calculator

Base = ROUNDUP(45 + 1.3 × Jumps) EP = ROUNDUP(Base × Event Character Multiplier × Jump Rope Multiplier) × HoloPass × Energy Multiplier

The featured Jump Rope event bonus is treated as its own ×1.5 multiplier. HoloPass is applied afterward as ×2. For example, with two event characters (+40%), featured Jump Rope, HoloPass, 0 energy, and 0 jumps: 45 × 1.4 × 1.5 = 94.5 → 95 × 2 = 190 EP.
